ELEPHANTINE TALES — ESTATE EXPLORER
A website design concept. Prepared September 2026.


HOW TO OPEN IT
--------------
Double-click  index.html
It opens in your web browser. Nothing to install.

Keep the whole folder together. The pictures live in the folders beside
index.html, so moving index.html on its own will leave you with a blank page.

You need an internet connection the first time you open it. The typefaces
and the animation library come from the web; everything else is in this folder.

Best viewed full screen in Chrome, Edge or Safari. It works on a phone too.


WHAT YOU CAN DO
---------------
Drag the map to move around it. Scroll or pinch to zoom in and out.

Click any glowing dot to open its card on the right. The card has a photograph,
a description, links to other places on the estate, and on the five main areas
a brown EXPLORE button that takes you into that part of the estate close up.

TODAY / PLANNED, top right. Switches between the land as it is now and the
estate as planned, with the villas, the pool and the lawns in place.

THE ROUND BUTTON, bottom right. Cycles Summer, Monsoon, Winter and Dusk.
On the whole estate and the clubhouse, Dusk brings up the evening view with
the lights on.

THE BAR ALONG THE BOTTOM. The house icon returns you to the whole estate.
The arrow opens the list of all six views.

PLOTS. In the three plot areas each numbered dot is a plot. Its card shows the
size, the area, which way it faces and an indicative price, with tabs for
photographs, the plot drawn to scale, the details and the building guidelines.
White dots are available, beige reserved, grey sold.

ENQUIRE, top right, opens the enquiry form.


STAND HERE, THE 360 VIEW
------------------------
Some cards have a STAND HERE button, or STAND ON THIS PLOT. It opens a full-screen
view from ground level. Drag to look around you. Scroll to zoom.

Where there is a chain of spots, arrows at the bottom step you forward and back,
the way Google Street View does.

These 360 views are free stock photographs of woodland, meadow and hillside. They
are there to show how the feature works. They are NOT the estate. Every one of them
has to be replaced with a real 360 photograph taken on the land.


PLEASE NOTE
-----------
This is a design concept, not a live website.

The 360 views are stock photographs, not the estate.

The enquiry form does not send anything. Nothing you type is stored or
transmitted anywhere.

The aerial views were produced for this concept from satellite imagery of the
estate. They show the real roads, ponds, clubhouse, farm and village in their
real positions, but they are illustrations, not survey drawings.

Plot positions, plot numbers, availability, prices and the descriptions of
amenities are invented to demonstrate how the site would work. None of it has
been confirmed with the estate and none of it should be quoted to a buyer.

Prices shown are extrapolated from the published starting price of
Rs 94 lakh for 21 cents and are marked indicative.

Before this goes live the aerial views need to be replaced with a commissioned
drone survey or a licensed image, and every fact needs to come from the estate.
